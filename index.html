7 × 7 Board

Event log

    Pieces move as queens and cannot pass through one another. Antiparticle pairs (K0/K0, K+/K−, π+/π−, μ+/μ−, e+/e−) annihilate on contact; π0 has no partner, and γ leaves the board as light. Repulsion reads the near ring — the eight squares a charged piece touches — and pushes a like charge found there one square outward into the outer ring. Attraction reads the outer ring — the eight squares two steps out — and draws an opposite charge found there one square inward into the near ring. Both happen at the same time, from the board as it was before either moved, so a particle shoved outward and one drawn inward can trade squares. Opposite charges already touching do not move, they bind; like charges in the outer ring do nothing. A step that would go off the board, or onto a particle that is staying put, does not happen — nothing is rerouted or slid sideways. Uncharged particles feel no field. Bound pieces are stuck: no player can move them again, but a later pull or push still drags them, and they may still decay. A decay needs enough empty squares for every product.
